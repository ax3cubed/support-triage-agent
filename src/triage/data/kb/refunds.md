# Refund policy

## Yearly plans
A yearly subscription can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge.

## Monthly plans
Monthly subscription charges are not normally refunded. Cancelling stops future charges.

## Duplicate or mistaken charges
A charge taken twice for the same period, or taken after a cancellation, is refunded in full.

## Who decides a refund
Every refund request is reviewed and decided by Ledgerly's billing team, including requests that look clearly inside or outside this policy. Nobody outside the billing team may promise, approve or refuse a refund. Refunds that are approved reach the card in 5 to 10 business days.
