# Clients not receiving invoice emails

## First checks
Open the invoice and check Activity: it shows whether the email was delivered, opened or bounced. Ask the client to check spam, and confirm the client's email address is spelled correctly on their client page.

## Send it again
Choose Resend on the invoice, or More › Copy link and share the link yourself. The link works without the email.

## A bounced email
A bounce means the client's mail server refused the message, usually because the address is wrong or the mailbox is full. Correct the address on the client page and resend.
