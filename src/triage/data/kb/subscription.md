# Managing your subscription

## Change plan or billing cycle
The business owner can upgrade, downgrade or switch between monthly and yearly under Settings › Billing. Only the owner can see or change billing.

## Cancel a subscription
The owner can cancel under Settings › Billing › Cancel subscription. The business keeps Pro until the end of the period already paid for, then moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted.

## Failed subscription payment
If a subscription payment fails, Ledgerly retries the card 3 times over 7 days and emails the owner each time. If all retries fail the subscription becomes past due and the business moves to the Free plan: reminders and bank sync stop and team members lose access, but no data is deleted. Paying the outstanding amount under Settings › Billing restores Pro immediately.

## Update the card on file
The owner can replace the card under Settings › Billing › Payment method. Never send card details by email or chat; Ledgerly staff will never ask for them.
