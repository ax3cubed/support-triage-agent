# Plans and pricing

## Free plan
The Free plan costs nothing. It includes 5 invoices per month and 1 user. It does not include automatic reminders, bank sync or team members.

## Pro plan
Pro costs £15 per month or £150 per year. It includes unlimited invoices, up to 5 team members, automatic reminders and bank sync.

## Free trial
Every new business gets a 14-day trial of Pro. No card is needed. When the trial ends the business moves to the Free plan unless the owner subscribes; no data is deleted.
