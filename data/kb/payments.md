# Online payments and payouts

## How clients pay
Clients can pay an invoice by card or bank transfer from the invoice link. The invoice is marked paid automatically when the payment succeeds.

## Processing fee
Online payments carry a processing fee of 1.5% of the amount paid, capped at £5 per payment. The fee is taken from the payout to your business. To have the client pay it instead, switch on Pass fee to client under Settings › Payments.

## When payouts arrive
Payouts are sent to your bank account on the next business day after the client pays. Payments made on a Friday, at the weekend or on a public holiday arrive on the next working day.

## A payout has not arrived
If a payout is more than 2 business days late, Ledgerly's billing team has to trace it with the payment processor. Support cannot trace payouts from the help centre.
