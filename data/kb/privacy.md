# Your data: export, deletion and privacy requests

## Export your data
An owner or admin can export invoices, clients and payments under Settings › Data › Export, as CSV or PDF. The export is emailed as a download link that lasts 24 hours.

## Delete your business or account
The owner can delete the business under Settings › Data › Delete business. There is a 30-day grace period during which the owner can restore it. Tax records are kept for 6 years where the law requires it.

## Privacy requests from people who are not account holders
If your details appear on an invoice sent by a Ledgerly customer and you want to see, correct or erase them, the request is handled by Ledgerly's privacy team (privacy@ledgerly.example). Formal privacy, legal or regulator requests from anyone also go to the privacy team.
