# Account access and security

## Reset your password
On the sign-in page choose Forgot password and enter your email. The reset link lasts 60 minutes. If it does not arrive, check spam and confirm you are using the email you signed up with.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on 2FA under Settings › Security. Save the recovery codes shown when you set it up. If you lose your phone, sign in with a recovery code and set 2FA up again.

## Lost phone and no recovery codes
Without the phone or a recovery code, access can only be restored by Ledgerly's security team after identity checks. Support cannot switch 2FA off.

## Suspected unauthorised access
If you see sign-ins, invoices or changes you do not recognise, the security team must be told at once so they can lock the account and review activity. This is always urgent.

## Changing the sign-in email
You can change your own sign-in email under Settings › Profile while signed in. A request to change the sign-in email for someone who cannot sign in, or that arrives from a different email address, always goes to the security team for identity checks.
