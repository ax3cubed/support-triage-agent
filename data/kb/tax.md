# VAT and withholding tax on invoices

## Add VAT to an invoice
Switch on VAT under Settings › Tax and enter your VAT number. New invoices then add VAT at 20% to each taxable line. You can mark a line as exempt when you add it.

## Withholding tax
If a client deducts withholding tax, record the payment and enter the amount under Withholding tax deducted. The invoice shows as fully paid and the deduction appears on your tax report.

## Tax advice
Ledgerly calculates tax from the settings you choose but does not give tax advice. Whether you must register for VAT, and which rate applies to you, is a question for an accountant or the tax authority.
