# Creating, sending and editing invoices

## Create and send an invoice
Go to Invoices › New invoice, pick a client, add line items and a due date, then choose Send. The client receives an email with a link to view and pay the invoice. You can also copy the link and share it yourself.

## Download an invoice as PDF
Open the invoice and choose More › Download PDF. On a phone the button is under the three-dot menu at the top right. Clients can download the PDF from the link they received.

## Edit an invoice after sending
A sent invoice that has no payments can be edited: open it and choose Edit. The client sees the new version on the same link. An invoice with any payment recorded against it is locked and cannot be edited.

## Correct a paid invoice
To correct a paid or part-paid invoice, issue a credit note from More › Issue credit note and then create a new invoice. Credit notes keep the audit trail intact; paid invoices are never changed in place.
