# Team members and roles

## Roles
Owner: full access, including billing, ownership transfer and deleting the business. Admin: everything except billing and ownership. Member: can create and send invoices and manage clients. Viewer: read-only.

## Invite a team member
Team members are part of the Pro plan (up to 5). An owner or admin goes to Settings › Team › Invite, enters the person's email and picks a role. The invitation expires after 7 days.

## Transfer ownership
Only the current owner can transfer ownership, under Settings › Team, by choosing Make owner next to an admin. Ledgerly never changes the owner on request from anyone else; if the owner is unreachable, the request goes to the security team for identity checks.
