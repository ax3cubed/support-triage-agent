# Automatic payment reminders

## Who can use reminders
Automatic reminders are part of the Pro plan. On the Free plan you can still send a reminder by hand from the invoice page with Send reminder, but nothing is sent automatically.

## Turn reminders on
Go to Settings › Reminders and switch on Automatic reminders. The default schedule sends one reminder 3 days before the due date, one on the due date and one 7 days after it. You can change the days or the wording of each reminder on the same page.

## Turn reminders off for one client
Open the client, choose Edit and switch off Send automatic reminders. Other clients are not affected.
