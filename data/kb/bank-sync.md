# Bank sync

## Connect a bank account
Bank sync is part of the Pro plan. Go to Settings › Bank accounts › Connect, choose your bank and approve access. Transactions from the last 90 days are imported and new ones sync every 6 hours.

## Sync has stopped
Banks ask you to approve access again every 90 days. If transactions stop arriving, go to Settings › Bank accounts and choose Reconnect.

## Duplicate transactions after reconnecting
Reconnecting can import some transactions a second time. Go to Bank accounts › Review duplicates and choose Merge all. Merging does not change any invoice or payment.
